Modern Greek musical legend, master of Entexna and Laika. Bridging Greek tradition with contemporary piano, voice, and original compositions.

The Musician

Teo Moulas blends the raw emotional power of Greek heritage with sophisticated arrangements of a world-class pianist. From intimate concerts to grand stages, his music tells a story of passion and artistry.

The Culture

He performs across genres, from Entexna and Laika to modern Greek pieces. His shows preserve tradition while engaging global audiences with melodic creativity and heartfelt performances.
